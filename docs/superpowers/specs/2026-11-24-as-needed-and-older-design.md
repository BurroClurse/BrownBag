# As needed from records, and taking an older prescription: short design

Approved by Tyler 2026-11-24.

## Why

Tyler: records that say "as needed" belong under As needed, and someone may still
take an older prescription, which staff never see.

## As needed

- On My medicines: records' (every direction has the field, and every current record
  merged into the card), marked ones, and additions whose How often is the list's
  As needed. Additions keep Edit and Remove. Records' have only Not taking: they
  aren't editable (Tyler), so there's no "I take it regularly".
- "Who says": "Your records say as needed", "You added this"; a marked one keeps
  "You marked: As needed, date".
- Staff page, See what's inside and the file: one part, "As needed", with "Records
  say as needed", "Patient says as needed" or "Added by patient". A note carries the
  records' at every code size. An older staff page shows it as a note.
- Not taking still wins. Words alone never move a record.

## I'm taking this

- On each older card. A popup, starting on Cancel: the medicine, what the records say
  and the date, "Only add it if you take it now. To restart a medicine, ask your care
  team first."
- The add form opens with the name only; what the old one said is shown beside it.
  The duplicate check runs. It's saved as the patient's own, "An older prescription,
  date", for staff too. The older card says "You're taking this".

## Approaches

Filling in the old dose (rejected: it could be saved unread). Moving the older record
itself (rejected: the records don't say it's current, the patient does).
