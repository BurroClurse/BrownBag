Brown Bag

Privacy

Last changed: November 24, 2026

Brown Bag is a student project by Tyler Beattie. It uses test patients only. Don't enter real health information.

What stays on your phone

Your list, what you add, and your sign-in are saved locked. While Brown Bag is open, your browser also holds your sign-in.

What Brown Bag's maker gets

Nothing from your list. There is no Brown Bag server, account, ads or tracking.

Who else is contacted

Your health records
For your medicines, allergies and insurance fills.
The National Library of Medicine
Gets medicine names you type and drug codes, but not your name.
GitHub
Hosts the app, and can see your internet address.

When you share

Your QR code is locked with 6 digits. Anyone with both can read it, and it doesn't expire. Someone with a picture of the code could work out the digits by trying them all. The person who opens it can save it as a file that isn't locked. You can save that file too. Share it only with people you trust.

Backups

Backups are locked. Where you save one is up to you.

Deleting it

Sign out ends the sign-in on this phone and keeps your list, locked. Start over erases Brown Bag on this phone. Delete your backups yourself.

HIPAA

HIPAA protects your records at your doctor and insurer. It doesn't cover the copy in Brown Bag.

If something goes wrong

If we learn your information was exposed, we'll say so here. Brown Bag has no way to contact you.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. Don't include health information.
